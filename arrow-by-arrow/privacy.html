Privacy Policy

Arrow by Arrow: Brain Puzzle, by SkGameDev
Package com.skgamedev.arrowbyarrow · Last updated 26 October 2026

Arrow by Arrow is a calm arrow puzzle game for Android. This page explains, in plain words, what information the game keeps, what our advertising partner Google collects, and the choices you have. “We”, “us” and “our” mean SkGameDev, the small studio that makes the game.

The short version
  • There is no account and no sign-in. We never ask for your name, phone number or email.
  • Your progress and settings stay on your phone. We never receive them.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob between levels. Google collects some device and usage data to show and measure those ads. Where the law needs it, you are asked first.
  • There is nothing to buy in the game.
  • You can play without internet. Ads need internet; the puzzles do not.

1. What the game keeps on your phone

To remember where you are, the game saves a small file in its own private storage on your phone. It holds:

  • The levels you have finished (and the fewest mistakes you made on each), the level you are on, a board you are in the middle of, and how many hints are left on the levels you have started, so you can carry on after closing the game.
  • Today’s daily puzzle if you are in the middle of it, and the dates of the daily puzzles you have finished (to show “Days played”).
  • Your settings: sound and music volume, mute, vibration, theme, less motion and thicker arrows.

This file is not sent to us or to anyone else. We cannot see it, and it is not linked to your name. Uninstalling the game removes it. If your phone backs up its apps to your Google account, Android may include this file in that backup; that backup is a feature of your phone, kept by Google under your own account, and we never see it.

2. Ads (Google AdMob)

Arrow by Arrow is free and is supported by ads from Google AdMob. Ads never interrupt a level: now and then a full-screen ad may show between levels, after you win, and your first levels have no ads at all. After them, when you run out of lamps, or have used a level’s three hints, you may choose to watch a short video for one more lamp or one more hint. There is no banner.

To show ads, the Google Mobile Ads software inside the game collects and sends to Google some information from your phone, such as:

  • Device identifiers, including your phone’s advertising ID and app set ID.
  • Your IP address, which can show your rough location (city or region, not your exact location).
  • Device and app details, such as phone model, Android version, language and app version.
  • How you interact with ads, such as which ads were shown and tapped, and when the app was opened.
  • Diagnostics, such as how quickly ads load and whether something went wrong.

Google uses this to deliver ads, measure how ads perform, prevent fraud and abuse, and, where you allow it, show ads based on your interests. Google acts under its own privacy policy. To learn more, see Google’s Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your consent. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, Google’s consent form appears before any ad is requested, and you choose whether to allow personalised ads. In some US states, Google’s privacy message lets you opt out of personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time in the game: Settings, then Ad privacy choices (this row shows wherever the law gives you that choice). In other places, Google may also show a consent or privacy message where local law asks for one.

Your advertising ID. You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your phone’s settings (usually Settings, then Google or Privacy, then Ads). After that, ads may still appear but are not tied to your old ID.

Ad settings we chose. We ask Google to show only ads suitable for a general audience (rated PG or lower). The game is not marked as made for children.

3. When you email us

Contact us in Settings opens your own email app with a message to us, with the game’s name and version in the subject. Nothing else is added, and nothing is sent unless you press send. If you write to us, we get your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to reply and to improve the game, we do not add you to any mailing list, and we delete the email when we no longer need it.

Privacy policy in Settings opens this page in your browser.

4. What we do not do

  • No account, sign-in or social login.
  • No purchases, and no payment details of any kind.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, files, camera or microphone.
  • No exact (GPS) location.
  • No chat, and no way to talk with other players.
  • No analytics service of our own, and no servers of our own: your game never talks to us.
  • We do not sell your personal information for money. Some US state laws count sharing data with Google for personalised ads as a “sale” or “sharing”; where they do, Google’s privacy message lets you opt out, and you can change your choice later in Settings, then Ad privacy choices.

5. Children

Arrow by Arrow is made for a general audience aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child under 13 has sent us information, please email us at skgamedev025@gmail.com. We will delete anything we hold, such as an email they sent us, and help you with the steps below to remove the rest.

6. Deleting your data

  • On your phone: uninstall the game, or open your phone’s Settings, then Apps, then Arrow by Arrow, then Storage, then Clear storage. This removes your progress and settings and cannot be undone.
  • Ad data: reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone’s settings. You can also see and manage your Google ad settings at myadcenter.google.com.
  • Emails you sent us: write to skgamedev025@gmail.com and ask us to delete them. We will reply within 30 days.

7. Security

Your game save sits in the game’s private storage on your phone, which other apps cannot read. Information sent by the Google ads software travels encrypted over the internet.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example India, the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or California), you may have the right to ask what personal information we hold about you, to correct it, or to have it deleted. Because the game does not send your progress to us, the only personal information we may hold is an email you sent us. For information held by Google, please use Google’s own tools linked above. To ask us anything, email us.

9. Changes to this policy

If the game starts to collect anything new, we will update this page before that version is released and change the date at the top.
