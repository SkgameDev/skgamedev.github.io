Privacy Policy

Big Crowd: Colour Fight, by SkGameDev
Package com.skgamedev.bigcrowd · Last updated 3 November 2026

Big Crowd is a colourful street game for Android: you send your crowd up the street with a colour cannon, grow it through gates and fill the other team's house with your colour. This page explains, in plain words, what the game keeps, what our ad partner Google collects, and the choices you have. “We”, “us” and “our” mean SkGameDev, the small studio that makes the game.

The short version
  • There is no account and no sign-in. We never ask for your name, phone number or email.
  • Your progress, coins and settings stay on your phone. We never receive them.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob. Google collects some device and usage data to show and measure those ads. Where the law needs it, you are asked first.
  • There is nothing to buy with real money. Coins are earned only by playing.
  • You can play without internet. Ads need internet; the levels do not.

1. What the game keeps on your phone

To remember where you are, the game saves a small amount of text in its own private storage on your phone (with a second copy, so a sudden power cut does not lose your progress). It holds:

  • The levels you have won, your stars, your coins, your upgrades, the cannons, colours and outfits you own and have chosen, and which tips you have already seen.
  • Your settings: sound, music, vibration and Battery saver.

This is not sent to us or to anyone else. We cannot see it, and it is not linked to your name. Uninstalling the game removes it. If your phone backs up its apps to your Google account, Android may include it in that backup; that backup is a feature of your phone, kept by Google under your own account, and we never see it.

2. Ads (Google AdMob)

Big Crowd is free and is supported by ads from Google AdMob. Ads never interrupt a level. Your first five levels have no full-screen ads at all; after that, a full-screen ad may show now and then after you win a level (at most once in three levels, never after a loss). You may also choose to watch a short video for a reward, such as double coins after a win. Watching is always your choice. There is no banner.

To show ads, the Google Mobile Ads software inside the game collects and sends to Google some information from your phone, such as:

  • Device identifiers, including your phone’s advertising ID and app set ID.
  • Your IP address, which can show your rough location (city or region, not your exact location).
  • Device and app details, such as phone model, Android version, language and app version.
  • How you interact with ads, such as which ads were shown and tapped, and when the app was opened.
  • Diagnostics, such as how quickly ads load and whether something went wrong.

Google uses this to deliver ads, measure how ads perform, prevent fraud and abuse, and, where you allow it, show ads based on your interests. Google acts under its own privacy policy. To learn more, see Google’s Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your consent. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, Google’s consent form appears before any ad is requested, and you choose whether to allow personalised ads. In some US states, Google’s privacy message lets you opt out of personalised ads. Where the law gives you this choice, you can change it at any time in the game’s Settings. In other places, Google may also show a consent or privacy message where local law asks for one.

Your advertising ID. You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your phone’s settings (usually Settings, then Google or Privacy, then Ads). After that, ads may still appear but are not tied to your old ID.

Ad settings we chose. We ask Google to show only ads suitable for a general audience (rated PG or lower).

3. When you email us

If you write to us, we get your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to reply and to improve the game, we do not add you to any mailing list, and we delete the email when we no longer need it. Privacy policy in the game’s Settings opens this page in your browser.

4. What we do not do

  • No account, sign-in or social login.
  • No purchases with real money, and no payment details of any kind.
  • No random prizes: everything in the Shop has a fixed price in coins.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, files, camera or microphone.
  • No exact (GPS) location.
  • No chat, and no way to talk with other players.
  • No analytics service of our own, and no servers of our own: your game never talks to us.
  • We do not sell your personal information for money. Some US state laws count sharing data with Google for personalised ads as a “sale” or “sharing”; where they do, Google’s privacy message lets you opt out.

5. Children

Big Crowd is made for a general audience. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child under 13 has sent us information, please email us at skgamedev025@gmail.com. We will delete anything we hold, such as an email they sent us, and help you with the steps below to remove the rest.

6. Deleting your data

  • On your phone: uninstall the game, or open your phone’s Settings, then Apps, then Big Crowd, then Storage, then Clear storage. This removes your progress and settings and cannot be undone.
  • Ad data: reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone’s settings. You can also see and manage your Google ad settings at myadcenter.google.com.
  • Emails you sent us: write to skgamedev025@gmail.com and ask us to delete them. We will reply within 30 days.

7. Security

Your game save sits in the game’s private storage on your phone, which other apps cannot read. Information sent by the Google ads software travels encrypted over the internet.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example India, the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or California), you may have the right to ask what personal information we hold about you, to correct it, or to have it deleted. Because the game does not send your progress to us, the only personal information we may hold is an email you sent us. For information held by Google, please use Google’s own tools linked above. To ask us anything, email us.

9. Changes to this policy

If the game starts to collect anything new, we will update this page before that version is released and change the date at the top.
