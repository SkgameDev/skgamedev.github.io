Privacy Policy

Drive Worlds: Car Simulator, by SkGameDev
Package com.skgamedev.drivingsim · Last updated 8 November 2026

Drive Worlds is a car driving game for Android: you drive and race ten cars across six worlds, in Free Ride or in races against cars driven by the game. This page explains, in plain words, what the game keeps and what it does not. “We”, “us” and “our” mean SkGameDev, the small studio that makes the game.

The short version
  • The game does not collect or share any personal data. Nothing about you or your phone is sent to us or to anyone else.
  • There are no ads in the game.
  • There is no account and no sign-in. We never ask for your name, phone number or email.
  • Your progress, cars, Credits and settings stay on your phone.
  • The game works offline. It does not use the internet at all.
  • There is nothing to buy with real money. Credits are earned only by playing.

1. What the game keeps on your phone

To remember where you are, the game saves a small file in its own folder on your phone (with a spare copy, so a sudden power cut does not lose your progress). It holds:

  • Your Credits, the cars you own and how you painted them, your number plates, the races you have finished and your best places and times, your achievements and your driving stats (distance and time driven).
  • Your settings: steering, gears, sound, traffic, graphics, camera view, and which tips you have already seen.

This is never sent to us or to anyone else. We cannot see it, and it is not linked to your name. Uninstalling the game removes it. If your phone backs up its apps to your Google account, Android may include it in that backup; that backup is a feature of your phone, kept by Google under your own account, and we never see it.

2. No ads, no analytics

This version of the game shows no ads and contains no advertising software. It has no analytics or tracking service, and the game engine’s own usage reports are switched off. The game has no servers of its own and never talks to us.

3. What the game uses on your phone

  • A light vibration tap when you press some buttons (Pause, Reset, View, Handbrake). It follows your phone’s own touch vibration setting.
  • The motion sensor, only if you choose “Tilt Phone” steering. This does not need any permission and nothing is stored or sent.

The game does not ask for and cannot reach your contacts, photos, files, camera, microphone or location.

4. When you email us

If you write to us, we get your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to reply and to improve the game, we do not add you to any mailing list, and we delete the email when we no longer need it.

5. What we do not do

  • No account, sign-in or social login.
  • No ads and no advertising ID.
  • No purchases with real money, and no payment details of any kind.
  • No chat, and no way to talk with other players.
  • No collecting, selling or sharing of personal information.

6. Children

Drive Worlds is made for a general audience. The game does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. If you are a parent or guardian and your child has emailed us, write to skgamedev025@gmail.com and we will delete that email.

7. Deleting your data

  • On your phone: uninstall the game, or open your phone’s Settings, then Apps, then Drive Worlds, then Storage, then Clear storage. This removes your progress and settings and cannot be undone.
  • Emails you sent us: write to skgamedev025@gmail.com and ask us to delete them. We will reply within 30 days.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example India, the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or California), you may have the right to ask what personal information we hold about you, to correct it, or to have it deleted. Because the game sends nothing to us, the only personal information we may hold is an email you sent us. To ask us anything, email us.

9. Changes to this policy

If a later version of the game starts to collect anything, or adds ads, we will update this page before that version is released and change the date at the top.
