Jolly Street: Kids Life World — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026. Developer: SkGameDev.

Jolly Street is a pretend-play game for young children. We built it to be safe: it works offline and we do not collect, store or share any personal information about your child or about you.

What the game keeps

The game saves its own progress (the town, the people made in the character maker, stars, stickers, pets and settings) only on your device. It is never sent to us. Deleting the app deletes it.

Photos

The camera button saves a picture of the game screen (only the game's own drawings, never the device camera) into the game's private folder on the device. The newest 12 are kept for the in-game album. They are never uploaded or shared, and the parents' page can delete them all.

Advertising

The game shows a full-screen ad now and then, only when the child travels to another place on the town map, never during play, never in the first five minutes, and at least three minutes apart. Ads come from Google AdMob, a Google Play Families certified ad network. Every ad request is marked as directed to children (COPPA and the GDPR age of consent), limited to ads rated G, and personalised (interest-based) advertising is switched off. Google may process limited technical information, such as the device's IP address and app information, to serve and measure ads, as described in Google's policies: policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

No purchases, no accounts, no chat

Nothing is sold and nothing is locked: new places open with stars earned by playing. There is no sign-in, no chat and no links out of the game for children. The parents' page and its privacy policy link sit behind a grown-up check.

Permissions

The game only uses internet access for ads. It does not use the camera, microphone, contacts or location.

Children's privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe information was collected, contact us and we will delete it.

Contact

Questions: write to the developer email shown on the game's Google Play page.
