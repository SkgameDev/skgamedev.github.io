Traffic Town: Car Jam Puzzle

Privacy policy · SkGameDev · last updated 19 November 2026

In short

Traffic Town does not ask for your name, email, contacts, photos or location, and has no accounts. Your progress (levels, stars, town repairs and settings) is saved only on your phone. The game shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google may collect some device information to show and measure those ads, as explained below.

What the game keeps

  • Your progress and settings, in a small file on your phone. It never leaves the phone and is removed when you uninstall the game.
  • We, the developer, receive no personal information from the game.

Ads (Google AdMob)

Between some levels the game shows a full-screen ad, and you can choose to watch a short video for an extra heart or hint. These ads come from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device type, rough location and how you interact with ads.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent through Google's consent message before any ad is requested, and you can change your choice later. You can also reset or turn off your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

Learn more: Google Privacy Policy · How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Children

Traffic Town is made for players aged 13 and up and is not directed at children under 13. Ads are limited to content suitable for a general audience (rated PG at most).
