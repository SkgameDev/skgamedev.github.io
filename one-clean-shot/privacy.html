Privacy Policy for One Clean Shot

Last updated: 2 November 2026

This privacy policy explains how the Android game One Clean Shot (Google Play package com.skgamedev.onecleanshot), made by SkGameDev, handles information. If you have a question, write to skgamedev025@gmail.com.

In short

One Clean Shot is an offline game. It has no account and no sign-in, it shows no ads, it sells nothing, and it does not use any analytics or tracking tools. We do not collect, store, sell or share any personal information about you.

What the game collects

Nothing. The game does not send any information about you or your device to us or to anyone else. It does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information.

What the game keeps on your phone

To remember your game, One Clean Shot saves a small file on your phone with:

  • your progress (missions finished, stars, coins, the Daily Puzzles you played and the days you played them),
  • the rifles and looks you have earned or bought with game coins,
  • your settings (aim speeds, Flip Up/Down, left-handed layout, sound volumes, vibration and other options).

This file stays in the game's own storage on your phone. We never receive it and cannot see it. If your phone's Android backup is turned on, Android may include this file in your own Google backup; that backup is handled by Google under your Google account, and we cannot see it either.

How long it is kept, and how to delete it

The save file is kept until you delete it. To delete it, uninstall the game, or open your phone's Settings > Apps > One Clean Shot > Storage > Clear storage. Because we hold no information about you, there is nothing for us to delete on our side, and there is no account to close.

Permissions

The game uses the phone's vibration for a short buzz when you fire (you can turn it off in Settings > Vibration). The Android package also lists internet access, which the game engine adds; the game itself does not use the internet and works fully offline.

Children

One Clean Shot is not made for children. It is meant for players aged 18 and over (its Google Play age rating may be lower, for example 16+). We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because we do not collect or hold personal information, there is nothing for us to show, correct, move or delete. If you still have a question about your privacy, write to skgamedev025@gmail.com and we will answer.

Changes to this policy

If the game changes in a way that affects your privacy (for example, if ads are added), we will update this policy before that version is released and change the "Last updated" date above. The newest version is always at https://skgamedev.github.io/one-clean-shot/privacy.html.
