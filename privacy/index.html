Privacy Policy — Beat Your Time

Last updated: 30 September 2026

Beat Your Time ("the game") is made by SK Bright Studios ("we"). This policy explains what information the game uses and why.

What the game collects

We do not collect, store on a server, or sell any personal information. The game shows ads from Google AdMob, which collects some information itself, as described below.

  • Your progress stays on your phone. Levels, times, stars, fish, pearls, the things on your home pond and your settings are saved only in the game's storage on your device. We cannot see them. Uninstalling the game deletes them.
  • No account is needed, and the game never asks for your name, email address, contacts, location, photos or anything else about you.
  • No analytics of our own. The game has no analytics or tracking tools beyond what Google AdMob uses to show ads.

Advertising

The game is free and shows ads from Google AdMob: now and then a full-screen ad between rounds (never during a round), and a video you can choose to watch for extra pearls. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, AdMob collects and shares with Google:

  • your device's advertising ID,
  • your approximate location, worked out from your IP address,
  • how you interact with the ads, and
  • diagnostic information, such as crash logs and performance data.

How Google uses this information is explained at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

  • Your consent. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, using Google's consent form. You can change your choice at any time from Settings → Privacy choices in the game.
  • Your advertising ID. You can reset or delete it in your phone's settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads on most Android phones). Ads you then see will not be personalised.
  • Ad content. The game asks AdMob only for ads suitable for general audiences.

Sharing and challenges

"Share" and "Challenge a friend" open your phone's own share menu with a short message you can read before sending. It holds your time, which pond you played and a link. The link carries only that level, a number that sets out the pond, and the times: nothing about you or your phone. Nothing is sent unless you choose an app and send it yourself.

When your friend opens a challenge link, it goes to a small web page that opens the game (or its Play Store page). The page uses no cookies and no analytics. The service that hosts it may keep ordinary server records, such as IP addresses, for a short time for security.

Notifications

If you allow the daily reminder, the game plans at most one notification a day on your phone. The phone creates it; nothing is sent to or from any server. You can turn it off in the game's Settings or in your phone's settings.

Children

The game is made for a general audience. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If the game changes what information it uses, we will update this policy and the "Last updated" date above.
