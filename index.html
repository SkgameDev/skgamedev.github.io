How I work

Every game here followed the same path, and I owned all of it: concept, prototype, core loop, levels, art and audio, a performance pass for low-end Android phones, ad integration, the store listing, a closed test, then release and updates.

I build with Unity and C#, ship as Android App Bundles, and monetise with Google AdMob — including consent flows and Families-policy-compliant ad setups for the children's titles. I automate the repetitive parts, from build scripts to self-tests that play through every level, so the time goes into the games instead of the process.
