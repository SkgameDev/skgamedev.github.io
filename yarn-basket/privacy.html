Privacy Policy

Yarn Basket by SkGameDev
Package com.skgamedev.yarnbasket · Last updated 24 October 2026

Yarn Basket is a calm wool puzzle game for Android. This page explains, in plain words, what information the game keeps, what our advertising partner Google collects, and the choices you have. “We”, “us” and “our” mean SkGameDev, the small studio that makes the game.

The short version
  • There is no account and no sign-in. We never ask for your name, phone number or email.
  • Your progress, coins and settings stay on your phone. We never receive them.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob. Google collects some device and usage data to show and measure those ads. Where the law needs it, you are asked first.
  • Purchases go through Google Play. We never see your card, UPI or bank details.
  • You can play without internet. Ads need internet; the puzzles do not.

1. What the game keeps on your phone

To remember where you are, the game saves a small amount of information in its own private storage on your phone:

  • Your level, coins and boosters (Undo, Extra spot, Magnet), and a level you are in the middle of, so you can carry on after closing the game.
  • Your wool blanket pictures, daily chest and streak, and festival progress.
  • Your settings: sound, music, vibration, daily reminder, bigger text and colour signs.
  • Which shop items you own (for example No ads), so they keep working.
  • A record of ad timing (for example, when the last ad was shown and how many videos you watched today), so the game can keep ads spaced out and give any reward it owes you.
  • Simple counts of game events, such as how many levels you started or won. These counts stay on the phone and are not sent to us or to anyone else.

This information is not sent to us. We cannot see it, and it is not linked to your name. Uninstalling the game removes it.

2. Ads (Google AdMob)

Yarn Basket is free to play and is supported by ads from Google AdMob. There is a banner on the Home screen, an occasional full-screen ad between levels, and optional videos you can choose to watch for a reward. There is no forced ad while you are playing a level.

To show ads, the Google Mobile Ads software inside the game collects and sends to Google some information from your phone, such as:

  • Device identifiers, including your phone’s advertising ID.
  • Your IP address, which can show your rough location (city or region, not your exact location).
  • Device and app details, such as phone model, Android version, language and app version.
  • How you interact with ads, such as which ads were shown and tapped.
  • Diagnostics, such as crash and performance information about the ads.

Google uses this to deliver ads, measure how ads perform, prevent fraud and abuse, and, where you allow it, show ads based on your interests. Google acts under its own privacy policy. To learn more, see Google’s Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your consent. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, Google’s consent form appears before any ad is requested, and you choose whether to allow personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time in the game: Settings, then Ad privacy choices. In other places, Google may also show a consent or privacy message where local law asks for one.

Your advertising ID. You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your phone’s settings (usually Settings, then Google or Privacy, then Ads). After that, ads may still appear but are not tied to your old ID.

Ad settings we chose. We ask Google to show only ads suitable for a general audience (rated PG or lower). The game is not marked as made for children.

No ads purchase. If you buy No ads, the banner and the ads between levels stop. Videos you choose to watch for rewards still come from Google AdMob.

3. Purchases (Google Play)

The game may offer optional purchases, such as No ads or coin packs. All payments are handled by Google Play’s billing system. We never see or store your card, UPI or bank details. When you buy something, Google Play tells the game which item was bought, so the game can give it to you, and the game keeps a note on your phone that it was given. Google Play keeps its own record of your purchase under the Google Payments Privacy Notice and Google’s Privacy Policy.

If you change phones, the Restore purchases button in Settings or the Shop asks Google Play for items you own, such as No ads.

4. Daily reminder (local notifications)

Settings has a Daily reminder switch. In versions of the game that support reminders, the game can show a reminder at most once a day, in the evening, for example when your daily chest is ready. These reminders are set by your phone itself. Nothing is sent from a server, and no information leaves your phone for them. On Android 13 and newer, your phone asks your permission before any reminder is shown. You can turn reminders off at any time with the Daily reminder switch, or in your phone’s notification settings.

5. When you email us

Contact us and Report a problem in Settings open your own email app with a message to us. To help us fix problems, the message is filled in with your level and level code, your phone model, your Android version and the game’s version. You can read and change the message before you send it, and nothing is sent unless you press send. If you write to us, we get your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to reply and to fix the game, we do not add you to any mailing list, and we delete the email when we no longer need it.

6. What we do not do

  • No account, sign-in or social login.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, files, camera or microphone.
  • No exact (GPS) location.
  • No chat, and no way to talk with other players.
  • No analytics service of our own. Your game counts stay on your phone.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

7. Children

Yarn Basket is made for a general audience aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child under 13 has used the game or sent us information, please email us at skgamedev025@gmail.com. We will delete anything we hold, such as an email they sent us, and help you with the steps below to remove the rest.

8. Deleting your data

  • On your phone: uninstall the game, or open your phone’s Settings, then Apps, then Yarn Basket, then Storage, then Clear storage. This removes all progress, coins and settings, and cannot be undone. Items you bought, such as No ads, can be brought back with Restore purchases.
  • Ad data: reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone’s settings. You can also see and manage your Google ad settings at myadcenter.google.com.
  • Emails you sent us: write to skgamedev025@gmail.com and ask us to delete them. We will reply within 30 days.

9. Security

Your game save sits in the game’s private storage on your phone, which other apps cannot read. Information sent by the Google ads software and by Google Play travels encrypted over the internet.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example India, the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or California), you may have the right to ask what personal information we hold about you, to correct it, or to have it deleted. Because the game does not send your progress to us, the only personal information we may hold is an email you sent us. For information held by Google, please use Google’s own tools linked above. To ask us anything, email us.

11. Changes to this policy

If the game starts to collect anything new, we will update this page before that version is released and change the date at the top.
