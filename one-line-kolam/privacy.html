Privacy Policy

One Line Kolam: Draw Puzzle, by SkGameDev
Package com.skgamedev.onelinekolam · Last updated 4 November 2026

One Line Kolam is a drawing puzzle for Android: you draw every line of a figure in one line, and your line turns into a kolam filled with rangoli colours. This page explains, in plain words, what the game keeps, what our ad partner Google collects, and the choices you have. “We”, “us” and “our” mean SkGameDev, the small studio that makes the game.

The short version
  • The game itself collects no personal information. There is no account and no sign-in.
  • Your progress, your kolams and your settings stay on your phone. We never receive them.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob. Google collects some device and usage data to show and measure those ads. Where the law needs it, you are asked first.
  • There is nothing to buy. There are no in-app purchases.
  • The puzzles work offline. Only the ads need internet.

1. What the game keeps on your phone

To remember where you are, the game saves a small amount of data in its own private storage on your phone. It holds:

  • The levels, daily kolams and festival kolams you have solved, and the way you drew each one, so My kolams can draw it again.
  • The colours you chose in “Colour it”, the kolam on your doorstep, your hints and which tips you have seen.
  • Your settings, such as sound, music, vibration, thicker lines and colour-blind colours.

This is not sent to us or to anyone else. We cannot see it, and it is not linked to your name. Uninstalling the game removes it. If your phone backs up its apps to your Google account, Android may include it in that backup; that backup is a feature of your phone, kept by Google under your own account, and we never see it.

2. Saving and sharing your kolams

When you tap Save picture, the game saves a picture of your kolam to your phone’s photos (in a folder called “One Line Kolam”). On Android 9 and older, your phone may ask you to allow the game to save files; it is used only to save that picture. When you tap Share, the game opens your phone’s own share menu with the picture and a short line of text, and you choose where it goes. The game does not send the picture anywhere by itself, and we never see it. The game does not read your photos.

3. Ads (Google AdMob)

One Line Kolam is free and is supported by ads from Google AdMob. Ads never interrupt a puzzle. The ad software does not start until you have solved level 5, and there are no full-screen ads in your first 11 levels. After that, a full-screen ad may show now and then after you win a level, when you tap Next (at most once in three levels). There is never an ad on Undo, Start again or Hint, never during the moment your kolam appears, and never around the daily kolam. You may also choose to watch a short video for 3 more hints. Watching is always your choice. There is no banner.

To show ads, the Google Mobile Ads software inside the game collects and sends to Google some information from your phone, such as:

  • Device identifiers, including your phone’s advertising ID and app set ID.
  • Your IP address, which can show your rough location (city or region, not your exact location).
  • Device and app details, such as phone model, Android version, language and app version.
  • How you interact with ads, such as which ads were shown and tapped, and when the app was opened.
  • Diagnostics, such as how quickly ads load and whether something went wrong.

Google uses this to deliver ads, measure how ads perform, prevent fraud and abuse, and, where you allow it, show ads based on your interests. Google acts under its own privacy policy. To learn more, see Google’s Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your consent. The game uses Google’s consent tool (the User Messaging Platform). If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, Google’s consent form appears before any ad is requested, and you choose whether to allow personalised ads. In some US states, Google’s privacy message lets you opt out of personalised ads. Where the law gives you this choice, you can change it at any time in the game’s Settings under Privacy choices. In other places, Google may also show a consent or privacy message where local law asks for one.

Your advertising ID. You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your phone’s settings (usually Settings, then Google or Privacy, then Ads). After that, ads may still appear but are not tied to your old ID.

Ad settings we chose. We ask Google to show only ads suitable for a general audience (rated PG or lower).

4. When you email us

If you write to us, we get your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to reply and to improve the game, we do not add you to any mailing list, and we delete the email when we no longer need it.

5. What we do not do

  • No account, sign-in or social login.
  • No in-app purchases, and no payment details of any kind.
  • No access to your contacts, files, camera or microphone, and the game does not read your photos.
  • No exact (GPS) location.
  • No chat, and no way to talk with other players.
  • No analytics service of our own, and no servers of our own: your game never talks to us. The daily kolam comes with the game and needs no internet.
  • We do not sell your personal information for money. Some US state laws count sharing data with Google for personalised ads as a “sale” or “sharing”; where they do, Google’s privacy message lets you opt out.

6. Children

One Line Kolam is made for a general audience. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child under 13 has sent us information, please email us at skgamedev025@gmail.com. We will delete anything we hold, such as an email they sent us, and help you with the steps below to remove the rest.

7. Deleting your data

  • On your phone: uninstall the game, or open your phone’s Settings, then Apps, then One Line Kolam, then Storage, then Clear storage. This removes your progress, your kolams and your settings and cannot be undone. Pictures you saved stay in your photos until you delete them.
  • Ad data: reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone’s settings. You can also see and manage your Google ad settings at myadcenter.google.com.
  • Emails you sent us: write to skgamedev025@gmail.com and ask us to delete them. We will reply within 30 days.

8. Security

Your game save sits in the game’s private storage on your phone, which other apps cannot read. Information sent by the Google ads software travels encrypted over the internet.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example India, the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or California), you may have the right to ask what personal information we hold about you, to correct it, or to have it deleted. Because the game does not send your progress to us, the only personal information we may hold is an email you sent us. For information held by Google, please use Google’s own tools linked above. To ask us anything, email us.

10. Changes to this policy

If the game starts to collect anything new, we will update this page before that version is released and change the date at the top.
